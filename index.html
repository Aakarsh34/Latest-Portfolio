I’m pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, specializing in Data Science, at SRM Institute of Science and Technology.

My projects move between retrieval systems, model evaluation, cloud deployment, and student-facing analytics. Across them, I’m interested in the same question: how do we make a complicated system feel clear and useful to the person on the other side?
